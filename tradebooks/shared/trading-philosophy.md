# Trading Philosophy

## Key philosophy

- Trading is not a zero-sum game.
- I am making some money because bigger players are making more money.
- It's not because someone else has to lose money.
- I don't see myself competing with other day traders.
- My strategy is to follow other big players and share a small piece of their profit.
- So my best strategy starts with big trade ideas and it's less about low-level technical analysis.
- We make money by following large players. And the large players are placing those trades to achieve their goals.
