# Big Offer Clear, Fail Back Below, Breakdown Bid

* Entry
  * Buyers cleared a big offer wall, then it continue to go up but the follow-through was weak/small.
  * Then price go back down below the preivous big offer wall. 
  * Then there needs to be some bid wall below the current price. It can be just a small bid wall, but there need to be bids at least.
  * Enter with short when it breakdown below the small bids. Wait for it to clear such bids, then short when
    * it makes a new low after a weak bounce
    * asks price is below the small bid wall
    * if there are no small bids, short the breakdown of low of day

This is the more strict version of big offer wall clear fail back below pattern, because after it fail back below, it waits for more confirmation of losing a small bid wall to trigger the entry.

The reason we need more confirmation is that it's normal for an uptrend stock to make some pullback after hitting a large offer wall. So when it pulls back after clearing a large offer wall, we don't know whether it's a pullback or reversal.

* Stop loss
  * The high of the failed breakout above the wall.
