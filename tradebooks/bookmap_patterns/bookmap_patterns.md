## Pending patterns

### Pattern: `Big Wall Reject, Go Down`

* Entry
  * Price pushes into a big wall and gets rejected there. Enter the short trade when price gets back below the wall and the rejection is clear.
* Stop loss
  * The rejection high, or just above the wall if the rejection stayed very tight.

![Bookmap big wall rejection example](../../data/processed/2026/04/21/UNH/bookmap_screenshots/image.png)



### Pattern: `Big Wall Double Test, Reject`

* Entry
  * Price rejects once from a big wall, then comes back to test the same wall again. Enter the short trade when the second test fails and price starts trading back below the wall.
* Stop loss
  * The high of the second failed test.
