# Big Offer Clear, Fail Back Below

* Entry
  * Buyers cleared a big offer wall, but it cannot hold above it. Enter the short trade when the ask price falls back below the old wall.
* Stop loss
  * The high of the failed breakout above the wall.


This patterns seems to have very low win rate. 
* maybe only trade it when it happens at a key level. 
* Or maybe we wait for the ask price to hold below the old wall for 2 seconds or more. There has to be a duration requirement.
* first drop below don't trade it, wait for a 2nd pop that formed a lower high
* take it when the long side has stopped out. when it break down below the swing low before the push that cleared the offer wall.
