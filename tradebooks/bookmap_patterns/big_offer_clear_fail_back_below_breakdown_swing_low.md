# Big Offer Clear, Fail Back Below, Break Down Swing Low

* Entry
  * Buyers cleared a big offer wall, and pulled back. If it breakdown below the swing low since previous push, short it.
  * This is basically the stop loss of long for those breakout walls patterns. Imagine for the breakout buyers, where they will place their stops, I place my short entries when buyers stop out.
* Stop loss
  * The high of the failed breakout above the wall.
